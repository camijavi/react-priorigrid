## 🧠 The Eisenhower Method

The Eisenhower Method (or Matrix) is a productivity and time-management framework designed to help you prioritize tasks by categorizing them into four distinct quadrants based on two metrics: **Urgency** and **Importance**. Instead of treating every item on your to-do list equally, this method forces you to identify what needs immediate action, what should be scheduled for later, what can be delegated, and what should be eliminated entirely.

## ✨ Features

* **Authentication:** Secure sign-in and sign-out functionality.
* **Comprehensive Task Creation:** Add tasks with a Title, Description, Due Date, and Status (`Pending`, `In Progress`, `Done`).
* **Unassigned Inbox:** By default, new tasks are created without a quadrant assignment, landing safely in your inbox for you to process.
* **Drag & Drop Interface:** Seamlessly drag and drop task cards from your unassigned list directly into the appropriate matrix quadrant. Not sure where it belongs? You can freely drag tasks between quadrants as priorities shift.
* **Task Management:** Once a card is assigned to a quadrant, you can view its full details, change its status, or delete it entirely.
* **Rewarding Completions:** Marking a task as `Done` triggers a celebratory success message before automatically clearing the task from your board.
